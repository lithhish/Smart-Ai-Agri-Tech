# Agmarknet & State Agricultural Marketing Board (Tamil Nadu & India)
Source: Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare (Agmarknet) & Tamil Nadu Agricultural University (TNAU DEMIC)
Document Title: Official Cash-Crop Market Price Information, MSP Benchmarks & Mandi Portals
Reference URL: https://agmarknet.gov.in
Publication Year: 2024

## Section 1: Official Agricultural Market Price Sources & Mandi Intelligence
- Official Daily Mandi Portal: The Directorate of Marketing & Inspection (DMI) operates the Agmarknet portal (https://agmarknet.gov.in) and e-NAM (National Agriculture Market - https://enam.gov.in). These platforms publish real-time daily modal prices across regulated APMC mandis nationwide.
- Tamil Nadu Price Dissemination: TNAU DEMIC (Domestic and Export Market Intelligence Cell) provides forecast prices and market arrivals for major agricultural commodities across regulated markets in Tamil Nadu (e.g., Coimbatore, Erode, Tiruppur, Vellore, Theni).

## Section 2: Minimum Support Price (MSP) & Fair Remunerative Price (FRP) Benchmarks
- Sugarcane Pricing: For sugarcane, the central government announces the Fair and Remunerative Price (FRP) payable by sugar mills per quintal of sugarcane, linked to a basic sugar recovery rate (standard benchmark ~ ₹315 - ₹340 per quintal depending on recovery %). State Advised Prices (SAP) may additionally be notified by state governments.
- Cotton (Medium / Long Staple): Regulated under Government of India Minimum Support Price (MSP) with procurement supported by the Cotton Corporation of India (CCI). Average benchmark ranges between ₹6,620 to ₹7,520 per quintal.
- Groundnut (in shell): Minimum Support Price (MSP) benchmark declared by the Government of India is approximately ₹6,377 to ₹6,783 per quintal.

## Section 3: Farmer Advisory for Fetching Real-Time Market Rates
- Because daily market auction rates fluctuate hour-by-hour in local APMC mandis based on daily moisture percentage, quality grade, and arrivals, farmers should check official live mandi rates via:
  1. Agmarknet Portal: https://agmarknet.gov.in
  2. e-NAM Mobile App & Portal: https://enam.gov.in
  3. Kisan Call Centre (Toll-Free): 1800-180-1551
  4. Regulated Market Committee (RMC) notices in your respective district mandi.
