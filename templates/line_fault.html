⚡ Line Fault & Safety Help

ESP-NOW Architecture: Battery-powered ESP32 nodes attached to electric poles communicate via 2.4GHz ESP-NOW to an on-farm Gateway ESP32, which forwards data over Wi-Fi.

Safety Note: Prototype nodes use isolated non-contact current coils, opto-isolated stepdown circuits, and ADXL345 vibration/tilt sensors. Never connect microcontrollers directly to high-voltage lines.
